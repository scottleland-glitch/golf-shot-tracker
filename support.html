Support

StrokeWise tracks every shot of your round and shows your strokes gained.

Need help or found a bug? Email atypicalscott@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings) and what happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my data saved?On your phone only. There's no account. Deleting the app deletes your rounds, so back them up first.

How do I back up my rounds?Connect Google Drive backup in Settings for automatic copies in your own Drive, or tap Back up rounds to save a backup file (for example to Files or iCloud Drive).

How do I move my rounds to a new phone?Back up on the old phone, then use Restore from backup on the new phone.

What is strokes gained?It compares each shot with how many strokes your benchmark (for example PGA Tour or D1 college women) would need from the same spot. Plus is better than the benchmark, minus is worse. Tap the small ⓘ buttons in the app for details.

Why does it ask for my location?Only for Courses near me, to sort courses by distance. It's used on your phone and never saved. You can search by name instead.

How do I share a round live?During a round, tap Share live on the hole screen and send the link. Tap Stop sharing to end it and remove the live copy.

How do I log practice?Open My Game, tap ＋ and choose Log practice, or tap Log this drill on a round's takeaway.
